I am currently a senior undergraduate student in mathematics at the University of Michigan. I am currently applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 admission.

My research interests lie broadly in the mathematical foundations of economic systems, with a particular focus on stochastic dynamics, information, optimization, and allocation. I am especially interested in how uncertainty and information shape the dynamics and outcomes of such systems, and how optimization and design can be used to shape allocation. On the applied side, I am especially interested in large-scale market design.

Email: xuanzheh[at]umich[dot]edu

I am open to research collaborations and thoughtful projects. You can reach out to me at xuanzheh[at]umich[dot]edu.
